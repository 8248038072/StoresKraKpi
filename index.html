HR Appraisal

11-KPI HR Appraisal - 100 Marks, with Special Recognition bonus, Memo/Disciplinary deductions, Final Score and Rating.

Memo deduction rule: Memo/disciplinary deduction is applied once at HR Final Score level. Auto HR data does not reduce Discipline, Policy Compliance or Behaviour KPI again.

Period Label tags Recognition, Memo and Manual Score records for this cycle (e.g. "Jul-2026"). Saving a Snapshot lets the Department Dashboard build a Monthly/Yearly trend.

Once a period is Approved & Locked, Save Snapshot will refuse to overwrite it - use Re-open for Revision first if it genuinely needs to change (reason required, fully audited).
